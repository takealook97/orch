## Interpretation caveats

- The sample is small (12 review pairs, 8 implementation pairs). It shows direction only; repeated measurement is needed to check variance.
- The review experiment's author is assumed (a planted bug). Self-preference bias toward code actually written by an OpenAI model was not measured.
- Differences in reviewer strength (codex-high-m medium vs. claude-medium-m medium) are confounded with the family effect.
- The parallel variant's wall clock can include waiting from the row cap. The sum of worker time is only an approximation of cost.
